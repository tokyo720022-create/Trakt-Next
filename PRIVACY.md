# Trakt Next Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026. This page describes what the app does with your data. It was written from what the app's code does; the developer should review it before relying on it as a legal document._

**Trakt Next** is an Android app by tokyo720022-create that lets you track movies, TV shows and anime with your Trakt account. It has no servers of its own, no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your phone
- Your Trakt login tokens, so you stay signed in. A short-lived random code is also kept while you log in.
- Your display choices: list layout and, if you turn on **Persist filter**, your filter.

While the app is open it holds your lists and history in memory so screens open quickly. It does not save copies of them on the phone. Android backup is turned off for the app, so the login tokens are not copied to a cloud backup or another device.

None of this is sent to the developer. **Log out** removes your login tokens at once. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

## Who the app talks to
- **Trakt** (trakt.tv, api.trakt.tv): you log in on Trakt's own page. The app then reads and changes your history, watchlist, ratings and custom lists on your behalf. Titles, posters and details come from Trakt. Trakt's own terms and privacy policy apply to that data.
- **Simkl and AniList**: not used in this version. This policy will be updated before they are added.

## Your choices
You can log out or uninstall the app at any time. To remove the app's access to your Trakt account, remove it under Connected apps in your Trakt settings.
